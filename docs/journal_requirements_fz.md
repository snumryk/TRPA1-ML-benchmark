## Scope

The journal accepts original research in physiology and related biomedical
fields. The TRPA1 manuscript should therefore foreground the physiological
and pharmacological meaning of the findings rather than present only an
algorithm comparison.

## Manuscript format

- Submit as Microsoft Word: `.doc`, `.docx` or `.rtf`.
- Times New Roman.
- Main text: 14 pt.
- Tables: 10 pt.
- Line spacing: 1.5.
- All tables and figures must be inserted in the manuscript after their
  first mention.
- Color figures are acceptable and preferred.
- Figure captions must not be embedded inside the figure.
- Tables must be numbered and titled; all columns must have headings.
- Maximum number of figures for an experimental article: **6**.

## Length

- Experimental article: no more than **20 A4 pages**.
- Approximate character limit: **40,000 characters including spaces**.
- The limit includes references, tables and figures.

## Required structure

1. UDC.
2. Title.
3. Authors.
4. Institution / affiliation.
5. Corresponding email.
6. Structured abstract and keywords.
7. Introduction.
8. Methods.
9. Results and Discussion.
10. Conclusions.
11. Acknowledgments.
12. Financial support.
13. References.

The submission package also requires author information, a signed license
agreement and a conflict-of-interest disclosure form.

## Abstract

- Required in Ukrainian and English.
- Maximum: **250 words** in each language.
- Should include relevance, aim, methods, main results and conclusions.
- Keywords: up to **10**.

## Methods and statistics

The description must allow reproduction of the work. The manuscript must
specify:

- methods and experimental/computational conditions;
- statistical methods;
- software used;
- confidence intervals or value distributions;
- SI units where applicable.

Numerical values should be rounded consistently with their uncertainty.

## Results and Discussion

- The journal uses a combined **Results and Discussion** section.
- Do not duplicate the same numerical results in text, tables and figures.
- Discussion should focus on interpretation of the main findings.

## References

- Maximum for an experimental article: **20 sources**.
- Number references in order of first appearance.
- Cite in the text with Arabic numbers in square brackets.
- Vancouver style.
- Include all authors, full article title, abbreviated journal title,
  volume/issue and pages or article number.
- Journal abbreviations should follow Index Medicus.
- Recent works should predominate.
- Unpublished materials are not accepted.
- Authors are fully responsible for reference accuracy.
